## Safety Model

Safety is **layered**, never single-point:

1. **Hard external constraints** (code + physical locks)
2. **Ethical Gyroscope** (internal evaluator)
3. **Authority Gate** (capability ≠ permission)
4. **Human presence requirement** for critical systems
5. **Append-only provenance log**
6. **Quarantine** for any self-modification candidates (future extension)

The internal gyroscope **cannot**:
- Disable safety systems
- Unlock physical STOP levers by itself
- Authorize weapons or full autonomy without human
- Bypass the Authority Gate
